Privacy Policy

LumaTales Comic Studio · Effective October 30, 2026

This policy describes how LumaTales Comic Studio handles information when you use the iOS app.

Who operates the app

Operator: Hoang Anh Tuan
Privacy contact: dodangkhoa6994@icloud.com

Using the app

The app lets you read comics, save favorites, keep your reading position, and unlock comics with coins. You do not need to create an account to use these features.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your favorites, reading progress and settings on your device. It also keeps a local coin ledger, including coin balance, unlocked comics, and transaction identifiers needed to recognize in-app purchases. This helps the app credit coins and avoid applying the same transaction more than once.

The app's clear-data option removes favorites, reading progress, and the app's URL cache. It does not remove the coin ledger, coin balance or unlocked-comic records. Device backup and removal of app data are governed by your device and Apple settings. We cannot guarantee that a consumable coin balance or unlocked-comic data will be restored or synced to another device.

In-app purchases

Coin purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. Apple handles payment and related purchase processing under its own privacy policy. The app receives information about the transaction, including its identifier, product identifier, verification and revocation status, and uses it to credit coins and maintain the local ledger. We do not receive your full payment card number through the app.

Apple privacy information

Links to other websites

The app can open the Apple standard end user license agreement and this privacy policy in your browser. Those websites receive information as described in their own policies and your browser settings.

Information you send us

If you contact us using the privacy contact above, we will receive the information you choose to include so that we can respond to your request. Please do not include payment card numbers or other sensitive information in your message.

Retention and deletion

The app retains the local information described above on your device until it is removed by the applicable app or device controls. The app's clear-data option does not delete the coin ledger. For questions about personal information you send us or a request to access or delete it, contact us using the privacy contact above. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features or data handling change, we may update this policy. We will make the updated policy available through the app and the App Store listing.
