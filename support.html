Quick help

How do I browse and read comics?

Browse the comic collection in the app and open a title to start reading. Comic content is included locally for reading on your device. Some titles require coins to unlock; check the coin cost shown in the app before confirming.

Where are my favorites and reading progress?

Your favorites and reading progress are saved on this device so you can return to your saved comics and continue reading. PanelPounce has no app account or developer-operated cloud sync, so these records do not sync across devices.

I purchased coins, but they haven’t appeared.

Check whether Apple shows the purchase as completed or still pending, then reopen PanelPounce with an internet connection. If the balance is still incorrect, email us with the coin package, purchase date, and Apple order or transaction ID if available. Keep the app installed while we investigate so your local wallet remains available.

Can I restore coins or unlocked comics?

Coins are consumable purchases. Your coin balance and comic unlock records are kept locally on this device. PanelPounce does not provide account-based restoration of completed consumable purchases or transfer this local wallet to another device. Deleting the app and its data removes these records, and reinstalling does not restore them through PanelPounce.

What does “Clear cache” do?

It removes temporary cached files covered by this setting. These files may be recreated as you use the app. It keeps your favorites, preferences, reading progress, coin balance, and comic unlock records.

What does “Clear app data” do?

It resets local favorites, preferences, reading progress, and cached files covered by the confirmation message. Your coin balance and comic unlock records stay on the device. This is different from deleting the app and its local data, which also removes the wallet and unlock records. Neither action requests a refund from Apple.
