Your library

Tessivell offers illustrated comics, free first-page previews, favorites and coin-based comic unlocks. Favorites, reading progress, preferences, coin balance and comic unlocks are stored on your device. The native reader does not require a reading account.

Apple purchases

Coin packs are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. The app uses Apple’s verified transaction information to save your coins and prevent a transaction from being credited twice. Tessivell does not receive your payment card details.

Purchases and device changes

The local coin ledger keeps transaction identifiers and coin credits to prevent duplicate delivery. The native reader does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. Removing the app may remove local data; restoring consumable coins or comic unlocks on another device is not guaranteed.

Tracking

The native comic reader does not include an advertising attribution SDK or request access to your contacts, photos or location.
