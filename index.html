Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026
Effective Date: October 15, 2026

PanelPounce is provided by Savkan Ceyhan ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you browse and read comics, save favorites, purchase coins, unlock titles, and use application settings.

1. Information Handled by the App

Information stored on your device

PanelPounce stores your favorite comics, reading progress, application preferences, cached files, coin balance, comic unlock records, and purchase transaction identifiers locally on your device. These records let the app remember your choices, resume reading, and avoid crediting or charging the same operation more than once.

The current app does not require an app account and does not send these local records to a developer-operated server. It does not include third-party advertising or analytics services, or use guest identifiers for developer-operated payment configuration or product queries.

Apple In-App Purchase

Coin purchases and product requests use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment authorization, billing, and the information needed to provide its services. The app receives product identifiers, prices, transaction identifiers, verification results, purchase status, and refund or revocation information when supplied by Apple. It uses these results to verify purchases, credit coins, and prevent duplicate credits.

We do not receive your full payment card number, card security code, or Apple Account password through the app. Apple's handling of information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your inquiry or privacy request. Do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, or card security codes.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide local comic browsing and reading, save favorites and reading progress, maintain coin balances and unlock records, verify Apple purchases, prevent duplicate transaction processing, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Sharing and External Services

Apple provides the App Store and In-App Purchase services and processes information under its own policies. If you open an external link from the app, the destination website receives the connection information needed to display its page and applies its own privacy practices.

This privacy policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address when you visit the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or contact forms.

We may disclose information we actually hold, such as support correspondence, when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, limited to the relevant purpose.

4. Storage and Retention

Local records remain in the app's storage until removed by the applicable app controls or deletion of the app's local data. The app does not provide its own cloud account or cross-device synchronization. Copies in device or system backups are controlled by your backup settings and provider; uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete existing backups.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address the inquiry, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations, and then delete it when no longer needed. Apple retains its own transaction records under its policies.

5. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

Clear cache: the in-app option removes temporary files covered by that control. Files may be recreated when you use the app again.

Clear app data: this resets local favorites, preferences, reading progress, and cached files covered by the confirmation message. It preserves your coin balance and comic unlock records on the device.

Remove all local records: deleting the app together with its local data removes the wallet and unlock records as well. Offloading the app may retain its data. Removing local data does not delete Apple's transaction records or request a refund. Finished consumable coin purchases are not restored through an app account, so deleting local records may result in loss of your balance or unlock records.

To ask about or request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, contact ettentu@outlook.com. We cannot remotely access or erase records held only on your device. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify a request. Where information must be retained for legal obligations or an unresolved matter, we will explain the applicable limitation.

6. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the app and our support communications. Local records are stored within the app's device storage, and purchase results are verified using Apple's StoreKit services. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and software up to date and protect access to your device.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated account or analytics service. Minors should use the app with guidance from a parent or legal guardian and obtain any authorization required before purchases. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should not have been collected, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will show an updated date. We will provide appropriate notice of material changes and obtain consent where required.
